## English version

The seven stage docs, in deal order (English translations of the Chinese originals):

- [01 · Market Selection](01-market-selection_en.md) — Lock in one first-stop market, with three hard reasons for picking it.
- [02 · Find Leads](02-find-leads_en.md) — Build a channel mix that reliably produces 20 real leads a day, and accumulate 200 raw names.
- [03 · Due Diligence](03-due-diligence_en.md) — Grade every lead A/B/C, throw out shell companies and scammers early, and put your best hours on buyers who can actually close.
- [04 · Outreach](04-outreach_en.md) — Make one proper first contact, and hand over a 3-touch follow-up plan with dates and a stop-loss line.
- [05 · Qualifying (Negotiation)](05-negotiation_en.md) — Get the three-piece set — specs, quantity, destination port — plus target price range, expected payment terms, and who they buy from now.
- [06 · Quotation & Negotiation](06-quote_en.md) — Hand in a quotation, a concession ladder, and a few ready-made lines, so that not one concession is given away for nothing.
- [07 · Order & Delivery](07-order-delivery_en.md) — Take the order safely to the finish line: sign the right contract, inspect the right goods, collect the right money, deliver the right documents.
