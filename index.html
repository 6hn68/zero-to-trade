一条线，四步走。One line. Four steps.

先分清该先打谁，再写第一封信，按节奏跟进，对方回了再报价。Know who to contact first, write the first message, follow up on a fixed rhythm, and quote only once they reply.

01 · lead_score.py

客户打分Lead scoring

粘贴或上传名单，按联系方式、来源、采购信号、公司成熟度打分，排成 T0–T3，再按市场规则封顶。Paste or upload a list. Each lead is scored on contact details, source, buying signals and company age, sorted into T0–T3, then capped by market rules.

开发信Outreach

按分级生成中英对照首触文案、200 字符内的领英附注和官网表单版，每份都带发前自查清单。Tier-based first-touch copy in English with a Chinese reference, a LinkedIn note under 200 characters, a web-form version, and a pre-send checklist for each.

跟进排期Follow-up plan

三次触达，日期排好，避开对方周末。对方回复或明确拒绝，计划立刻停。Three touches with real dates, clear of the buyer's weekend. A reply or a clear no stops the plan at once.

    几条不变的规矩。A few rules that don't change.
    

    

    先易后难Easy first
    已经在聊的先回，决策人有采购信号的先打，纯冷名单批量轻触。Reply to live conversations first, then decision makers with buying signals, then cold lists in light batches.
    

    报价放最后Price comes last
    首触只问规格和年用量。先要小东西，再谈价。The first touch asks only for sizes and yearly volume. Small asks first, price later.
    

    三次就停Stop after three
    第三次仍没回应就停手进长名单，季度扫一次。No reply after the third touch: stop, move it to the long list, scan once a quarter.
    

    让步换条件Trade, don't drop
    每降一档价，都换来一个柜量、定金或独家条件。Every price step down buys a container, a deposit or an exclusive deal.
    

    没核实就写没核实Unverified means unverified
    不在目标清单的市场照常打分，但标“没核实”。Markets off the target list are scored as usual but marked unverified.
    

    数据留在本地Data stays local
    示例数据全是虚构公司和保留域名；提交前用 check_private_data.py 自查。Sample data uses made-up companies and reserved domains; run check_private_data.py before every commit.
